## Slide 2: The Reality Today

### Header
> **The Reality Today: Industrial Data is Enormous, High-Speed, and Trapped**

### Subtitle
> Millions of Sensor Readings Flow Daily — But Manual Tools Can't Handle the Scale

### Visual Layout Recommendation
* **Layout**: Left side displays high-volume data sources; right side explains why large-scale sensor data breaks manual workflows and requires MLOps.

### On-Slide Content

#### 1. Industrial Data is Huge and Fast
* Continuous 24/7 telemetry from SCADA, PLCs, turbines, and compressors.
* Hundreds of sensor tags generating **millions of rows of data every single day**.
* Fragmented across multi-GB historian archives, exported CSVs, and plant spreadsheets.

#### 2. Manual Workflows Break Down at Scale
* Traditional spreadsheets and manual scripts crash or take weeks on high-frequency sensor streams.
* Engineers spend months trying to align timestamps and join massive multi-table files.
* Critical equipment failure signals get buried in massive data noise.

#### 3. Why an MLOps Platform is Required
* Big sensor data requires **automated pipelines**—not manual, one-off scripts.
* An MLOps platform automates data ingestion, large-scale cleaning, model training, and real-time monitoring.

> 🚨 **The Bottom Line**: Large data creates a massive operational bottleneck. Without automated MLOps, 90% of industrial sensor data goes to waste.

### Presenter Talking Points
> *"Industrial plants generate millions of sensor readings every day from SCADA systems, PLCs, and heavy machinery. The problem isn't just that data lives in different files—it's that the volume and speed are too large for manual tools or spreadsheets. Manual workflows break under this weight. That is why an MLOps platform like AIConnex is necessary: it automates the heavy lifting of large-scale data ingestion and model building so you can turn massive sensor streams into real-time business value."*
